About Event
​THE ALIX HACKATHON

​Agents of Administration

​AI for the work no one can afford to get wrong.

​Join San Francisco’s sharpest builders for a 2-day sprint focused on high-stakes, paperwork-heavy, and emotionally complex processes.

​You’ll work on real problems where better software can reduce administrative burden, improve decisions, and create meaningful outcomes. Bring your technical skills, creative energy, and business instincts—and leave with something real.

​Alix HQ, San Francisco

​Monday, July 27 at 10:00 AM – Tuesday, July 28 at 4:00 PM

​Teams of 2-3 builders

​Registration requires approval

​The Challenge

​Choose from three real-world project tracks revealed at kickoff

​Each track will explore an administrative process where AI can make a measurable difference. Teams will build a working solution and present it live to the judges.

​No API credits are required or provided.

​Who Should Join

​This event is open to builders of any experience level who:

​Can attend in person in San Francisco

​Are excited about building with AI

​Take a tech-forward approach to solving problems

​Want to work on consequential, real-world challenges

​Register solo or with others. We’ll match participants onsite into teams of two or three.

​What to Expect

​-Real problems

​-Team formation and mentorship

​-Speakers and judges

​-Meals, snacks, and refreshments

​-Alix Hackathon T-shirts

​-Prizes and recruiting opportunities

​Food will be provided throughout the event, with dietary restrictions accommodated.

​Prizes

​$4,000 total prize pool

​Best Overall — $2,500
Audience Favorite — $1,000
Best Demo — $500

​Prizes are awarded per team.

​Participants can be fast-tracked to the final interview round at Alix.

​Projects will be evaluated across five areas:

​• Real-World Impact

​• Technical Execution

​• Product Quality

​• Originality

​• Demo
